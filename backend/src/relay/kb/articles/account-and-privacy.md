## Updating your details
You can change your name, email address and saved addresses in your account settings. Our support assistant can see your account but can't change your login email or password.

## Who can see your orders
Only you can see your orders. For your security, our support team can't discuss an order with anyone except the account holder, even a family member.

## Your data
We use your data to process orders and provide support. You can ask for a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account from the privacy page.
