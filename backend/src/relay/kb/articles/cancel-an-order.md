title: Cancelling an order
category: orders
---
## Orders that haven't shipped
If your order is still pending or processing, ask our support team and we will try to stop it before it leaves the warehouse. Cancellations are handled by a team member rather than automatically, because the warehouse may already be packing it.

## Orders that have shipped
A shipped order can't be cancelled. Once it arrives, you can return eligible items within {{returns.window_days}} days of delivery for a refund.

## Charges after cancelling
A cancelled order is never charged. If you see a pending authorization from your bank, it drops off within a few business days.
