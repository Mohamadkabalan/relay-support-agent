---
title: Changing the shipping address on an order
category: shipping
---
Whether you can change where an order goes depends on how far along it is.

## Before the order ships
While an order is pending or processing, the address can be changed right away. Our support assistant will show you the new address and ask you to confirm it before saving.

## After a label is created or while in transit
Once the carrier has the parcel, we have to ask the carrier to reroute it. These requests are reviewed by our team, usually {{approvals.expected_decision}}, and you will hear back in the same conversation.

## Out for delivery or delivered
At this point it is too late to redirect the package. If it can't be delivered, the carrier returns it to us.

## New address requirements
The new address must be in the United States. We need a full street address, city, two-letter state code and ZIP code.
