## Our support assistant
The chat assistant can look up orders, track packages, change addresses, start returns and issue refunds, any time of day.

## Talking to a person
Ask the assistant for a human at any point. It passes your conversation to our team with a summary, so you don't have to repeat yourself. We reply {{escalation.response_time_text}}.

## What a person handles
Our team handles missing deliveries, disputes about a decision, cancellations, and anything the assistant can't resolve.
