We're sorry when that happens. Here is how we make it right.

## Report it within the window
Report the problem within {{returns.window_days}} days of delivery ({{returns.window_days_refurbished}} days for refurbished items). Tell our support assistant which item it is and what's wrong, for example a cracked screen or a device that won't turn on.

## Refunds for damaged items
We refund the item price to your original payment method. You don't have to send the item back first. Refunds up to {{refunds.auto_approve_limit_cents | money}} are issued right away after you confirm. Larger refunds are reviewed by our team, usually {{approvals.expected_decision}}.

## After the window
After the window, a defect may still be covered by the manufacturer warranty. See "Manufacturer warranty claims".
