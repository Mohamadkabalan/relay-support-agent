title: Tracking says delivered but the package isn't there
category: shipping
---
## Check the usual places
Look around the delivery area, with neighbors, at a building mailroom or front desk, and in your mailbox. Carriers sometimes mark a package delivered a few hours before it actually arrives.

## Wait one business day
Most "delivered but missing" packages turn up within a day.

## Still missing?
Contact our support team. A team member will open a case with the carrier. Because these cases need an investigation, they are handled by a person rather than refunded automatically, and we will reply {{escalation.response_time_text}}.
