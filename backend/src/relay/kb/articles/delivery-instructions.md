---
title: Adding delivery instructions or a gift note
category: orders
---
## Order notes
At checkout you can add a note with delivery instructions, such as a gate code or "leave with the concierge", or a gift message. The note is shared with the warehouse and printed on the packing slip.

## Changing a note
Notes can't be edited after the order is placed. For an urgent delivery instruction, contact the carrier with your tracking number.

## Gift receipts
Gift messages are printed without prices. Gift recipients can return eligible items within the normal return window, and the refund goes to the original payment method.
