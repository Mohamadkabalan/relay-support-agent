## Buying gift cards
Gift cards come in $25 and $100 values and are delivered by email.

## Using a gift card
Enter the code at checkout. Any remaining balance stays on the card for future orders, and gift cards never expire.

## Returns and refunds
Gift cards can't be returned or exchanged for cash, except where the law requires it. A lost gift card code can be resent to the buyer's email address.
