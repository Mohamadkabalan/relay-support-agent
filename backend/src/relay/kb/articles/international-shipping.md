---
title: Do you ship outside the United States?
category: shipping
---
## United States only
We currently ship only to addresses in the United States, including Washington, DC and Puerto Rico. We can't send an order, or redirect one, to another country.

## Military and freight forwarders
We don't ship to APO, FPO or DPO addresses, or to freight forwarding services.

## Buying from abroad
You can place an order with a US card and a US shipping address, for example for a friend or relative in the United States.
