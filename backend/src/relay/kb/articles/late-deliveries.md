---
title: When an order arrives late
category: shipping
---
Every order shows a promised delivery date. Sometimes carriers miss it.

## Shipping refunds for late orders
If your order was delivered {{refunds.late_delivery_grace_days}} or more days after the promised date, we refund the shipping fee you paid. Ask our support assistant and it can check the dates and issue it. The refund goes to your original payment method and arrives in {{refunds.settles_in}}.

## Limits
The shipping fee is refunded once per order. Orders that shipped for free have no shipping fee to refund. The item price itself is not refunded for a late delivery, but you can still return the item within the return window if you no longer want it.

## Orders that haven't arrived yet
If the promised date has passed and the package is still moving, check the tracking. A late-delivery refund is possible once it is delivered.
