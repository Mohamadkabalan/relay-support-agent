---
title: Items that can't be returned
category: returns
---
Some items can't be returned for a change of mind.

## The list
- Final-sale items, including open-box clearance products. They are marked "final sale" on the product page and in your order.
- Gift cards.
- Digital products, such as game codes and VoltCare protection plans.

## If one of these arrives damaged
A final-sale item that arrives damaged or doesn't work can still be refunded as damaged or defective, if you report it within {{returns.window_days}} days of delivery. Gift cards and digital products can't be refunded as damaged; if a code doesn't work, ask for a person and our team will look into it.
