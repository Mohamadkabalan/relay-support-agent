## Open-box items
Open-box products were returned by another customer in like-new condition. They are sold at a discount and as final sale.

## Final sale
Final-sale items can't be returned for a change of mind. Please check the product details before you buy.

## Damage and defects
If a final-sale item arrives damaged or doesn't work, tell us within {{returns.window_days}} days of delivery and we will refund it as damaged or defective.
