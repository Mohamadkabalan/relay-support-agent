---
title: I didn't get an order confirmation email
category: orders
---
## Check your spam folder
Confirmation emails come from orders@pixelvolt.example. Search for that address and check spam or promotions folders.

## Check the email on your account
The confirmation goes to the email address on your account. You can see it in your account settings.

## Your order is still placed
If the order appears in your order history, it went through, even without the email. Our support assistant can confirm the details.
