---
title: What your order status means
category: orders
---
Every order moves through a few statuses. You can see the current one in your order history or by asking our support assistant.

## Pending
We have your order and are confirming payment. Most orders leave this status within a few hours.

## Processing
Payment is confirmed and our warehouse in Secaucus, New Jersey is picking and packing your items. You can still change the shipping address at this stage.

## Shipped
The carrier has your package, or a shipping label has been created and pickup is scheduled. Tracking updates appear as the carrier scans the parcel. "Out for delivery" means it is on the truck today.

## Delivered
The carrier has confirmed delivery. The return window starts on the delivery date.

## Cancelled
The order was cancelled before it shipped and you were not charged.
