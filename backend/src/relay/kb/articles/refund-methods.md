---
title: Where refunds are sent
category: refunds
---
## Original payment method only
Every refund goes back to the card used for the order. We can't send a refund to a different card, a bank account, a gift card or another person, even on request. This protects you if someone else gets access to your account.

## If the card has expired or been replaced
Banks usually pass refunds to the replacement card automatically. If that doesn't happen, your bank can help you locate the refund.

## Gift card payments
The part of an order paid with a gift card is refunded to a new gift card.
