## Refunds for returns
We refund a return when the warehouse receives it, which is usually 3 to 7 days after you drop it off. You'll get an email when the refund is issued.

## Other refunds
Refunds for damaged items, lost packages or late deliveries are issued as soon as they are approved.

## How long the bank takes
Once issued, a refund takes {{refunds.settles_in}} to appear on your statement, depending on your bank.

## Refunds under review
Refunds over {{refunds.auto_approve_limit_cents | money}} are reviewed by our team, usually {{approvals.expected_decision}}. You can keep chatting while you wait; the decision appears in the same conversation.
