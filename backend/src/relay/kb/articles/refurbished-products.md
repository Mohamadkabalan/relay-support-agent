## What refurbished means
Refurbished products are inspected, tested and repackaged by our technicians. They may show light cosmetic wear and are marked "(Refurbished)" in the product name.

## Return window
Refurbished items have a shorter return window: {{returns.window_days_refurbished}} days from delivery instead of {{returns.window_days}}.

## Warranty
Refurbished items carry a 90-day Pixel & Volt warranty against defects. VoltCare protection plans are available for most refurbished devices.
