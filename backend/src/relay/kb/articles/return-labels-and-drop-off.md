---
title: Return labels and dropping off your parcel
category: returns
---
## Printing the label
The prepaid label link arrives with your RMA number. Print it and tape it over any old shipping labels on the box.

## Packing
Pack the items with their accessories. Any sturdy box works if you no longer have the original packaging.

## Drop-off
Drop the parcel at any location of the carrier named on the label. Keep the drop-off receipt until your refund arrives.

## No printer?
Many carrier stores can print the label from the link on your phone.
