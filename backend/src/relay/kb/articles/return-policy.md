Changed your mind? Most items can be returned.

## Return window
You can return new items within {{returns.window_days}} days of delivery, and refurbished items within {{returns.window_days_refurbished}} days. We count calendar days in US Eastern time, starting from the delivery date. The last day of the window is included.

## Condition
Return items with their original accessories and packaging if you still have them. Items damaged by misuse may be refused.

## What can't be returned
Final-sale items, gift cards and digital products can't be returned. See "Items that can't be returned".

## Refunds for returns
Once the warehouse receives your return, we refund the item price to your original payment method. The original shipping fee is not refunded for change-of-mind returns.
