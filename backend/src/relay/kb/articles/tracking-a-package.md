---
title: Tracking your package
category: shipping
---
Once your order ships we email a tracking number from UPS, FedEx or USPS. The same number appears on the order in your account.

## Reading tracking updates
Each scan shows where the parcel was and when: label created, picked up, arrived at or departed from a carrier facility, out for delivery, and delivered. Gaps of a day or two between scans are normal, especially over weekends.

## Estimated delivery date
The estimate comes from the carrier and can move. Your order confirmation also shows the delivery date we promised when you ordered.

## When tracking stops updating
If there has been no new scan for {{refunds.lost_package_days_without_scan}} days, the package is treated as lost and you can ask for a refund. See "Lost packages and stalled tracking".
