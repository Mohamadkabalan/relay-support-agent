## What's covered
VoltCare covers defects and accidental damage, such as drops and spills, for two years from the date of purchase.

## Making a claim
Visit the VoltCare claims page with your order number. Repairs are usually completed within 10 business days.

## Cancelling a plan
VoltCare plans are digital products, so they can't be returned like physical items. To cancel a plan, contact our support team.
