## Coverage
New products come with the manufacturer's warranty, usually one year from delivery. It covers defects in materials and workmanship, not accidental damage.

## Making a claim
Contact the manufacturer with your order number and a description of the problem. The order confirmation email works as proof of purchase.

## Inside the return window
If the item is still inside our {{returns.window_days}}-day return window, it's faster to ask us for a damaged-or-defective refund instead.

## Protection plans
VoltCare protection plans extend coverage and include accidental damage. See "VoltCare protection plans".
